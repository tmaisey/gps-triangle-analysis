Thermalling turn-radius distribution

How tightly each pilot circles in a climb, Bill vs the round winner, pooled across the 14 distance rounds.

BillRound winner
The clearest, cleanest finding — and it ties to a lever. In sustained climbs Bill circles distinctly wider: median radius 38.0 m vs the winner's 29.2 m (~30% wider), from 4,464 vs 5,810 circling samples. The whole leader curve is shifted left. A tighter core keeps the glider in the strongest part of the thermal, which lifts climb rate and buys laps — a direct, coachable lever. This confirms the earlier 39/33 m turn-radius finding.

Per-point turn radius r = v / ω (ground speed ÷ bearing rate) during detected sustained climbs — runs of continuous circling ≥12 s, ≥270° of turn and net height gain — pooled over the 14 distance rounds. Curves are densities over 5 m bins; dashed lines mark each pilot's median (computed over the full circling set). Display window 0–100 m.
