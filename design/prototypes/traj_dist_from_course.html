Split result — supported only for the big excursions. On the typical point the leader sits closer to the course, not further: pooled median 37.0 m vs Bill 46.8 m, and Bill is the wider of the two on median in 11 of 14 rounds. But the leader's tail is fatter — mean 77.3 m vs 67.9 m, 11.0% of time beyond 200 m vs Bill's 6.2%, and the leader ranges further on mean in 8/14 and on the 90th percentile in 9/14. So: the winner hugs the line more of the time and commits to bigger climbs when they count.

Pooled over all ~1 Hz scored track points across the 14 distance rounds (Bill 19,870 pts, leader 23,180 pts); one point per second, so the curve is time-weighted. Distance-from-course = straight-line distance in the local metre grid to the nearest of the three triangle legs. Curves are unfilled densities (20 m bins). Leader = Bill's same-air group winner for that round.
