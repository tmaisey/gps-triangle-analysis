# Equipment / Airframe note — Start Energy section

_Read-only research note. GPS Triangle World Masters, Oschatz 2026, Sport class._

## Note (for the Start Energy section)

The top of the Oschatz 2026 Sport-class field is an airframe mix, not a monoculture: event winner Florian Griese, Philip Kolb and Ralph Losemann all fly the Pike Paradigm (Samba Model) — the same airframe Bill Maisey flies — while the rest of the leading group spans the ChocoFly Phantom / Phantom GPS (Oliver Ladach, Daniel Aeberli), the SolarWings SkyTouch GPS (Andrej Vrecer) and the ChocoFly Apollo (Jens Geider). Because the winner is on Bill's exact airframe, the ~2,900-point gap between them is a technique and task-execution gap, not an equipment gap. This is reinforced by the class rules, which cap every Sport-class glider at a 5 m span, 7 kg all-up weight and 75 g/dm² wing loading, compressing the performance envelope so that no legal airframe holds a decisive edge (gps-triangle.net Sport regs v1.6). As general aerodynamics — not a measured spec difference in this field — a cleaner or more heavily ballasted airframe can carry more speed on the fast legs and hold a flatter glide slope (higher L/D), while lighter wing loading tightens turn radius and helps in weak lift, all trade-offs a pilot dials in with ballast and flap presets rather than gains fixed in the airframe. Verified specifics are limited: the Pike Paradigm spans ~4.73 m and takes up to ~2.3 kg of ballast (Samba / f3j.com), the SkyTouch GPS quotes a best-glide speed of ~10.6 m/s and a competition speed range of ~55–90 km/h (solarwings.cz), and the Apollo 46 spans 4.6 m at ~93 dm² wing area (f3xvault); published glide-ratio (L/D) figures for these specific models were not found.

## Facts with citation

- **Sport-class limits (shared cap on all airframes):** max 5 m span, max 7 kg AUW, max 75 g/dm² wing loading. Source: GPS Triangle Sport-class regulations v1.6 — https://gps-triangle.net/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/regulations_sport_en_V1.6_Release01.pdf ; corroborated by https://silent-flight-tech.bmfa.uk/specialisms/gps
- **Pike Paradigm (Samba Model), Bill's airframe:** wingspan 4726 mm, three-part wing, full Rohacell core / CW59 carbon construction, ballast capacity up to ~2300 g. Sources: https://f3j.com/blogs/news/pike-paradigm-gps-sport-model/ , https://www.leomotion.com/en/product/pike-paradigm-gps-v-tail-rtf-4726mm-by-samba , https://www.modelflight.com.au/pike-paradigm-gps-version-1a-cw59-carbon-gps-triangle-sports-class-glider.html
- **SkyTouch GPS (SolarWings):** span increased to the 5 m class limit; best-glide speed 10.6 m/s (~38 km/h); competition speed range ~55–90 km/h; Reynolds range Re ≈ (1.9–3.3)×10⁵; V-tail (lower wetted area / drag vs X-tail per maker). Sources: https://www.solarwings.cz/en/skytouch-gps-sport-class , https://www.solarwings.cz/en/technique/skytouch-gps-settings
- **Apollo 46 (ChocoFly GmbH):** wingspan 460 cm, wing area 93.33 dm², AUW ~4.9–7.0 kg (implying ~52–75 g/dm² wing loading at that area), three-piece wing (carbon / spread-tow / fibreglass), removable V-tail. Source: https://www.f3xvault.com/?action=plane&function=plane_view&plane_id=662

## Caveat

Any performance claim in the note is either cited to a source above or explicitly flagged as general aerodynamics. The core, well-supported point stands on the field data plus the spec-capped class rules: the winner and Bill share the same airframe, so the gap is not attributable to kit.
