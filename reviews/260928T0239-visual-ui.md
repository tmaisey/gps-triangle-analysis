## Verdict

**Pass with fixes.** Structurally and behaviourally the report is sound: every page and all 18
Analysis views render, the view swap, dropdown, burger, collapsibles, anchors and all 50
cross-page links work at both widths, there is **zero horizontal overflow at any width tested**,
**zero console errors**, **zero uncaught exceptions**, and **zero external network requests** —
self-containment is confirmed empirically. The design constraints hold: white background, inlined
Spectral/Source Sans 3 both loaded (no fallback rendering), no accent bars, no gradient heroes, no
emoji.

No blockers. The defects are all in the **chart layer**: SVG labels clipped by the outer
`<svg>` viewBox on several charts (one axis tick disappears entirely), a legend/axis-tick
collision, inconsistent chart widths inside one content column, and — the most consequential —
chart text rendering at an effective **5–6 px on mobile**, which makes every chart's axes
unreadable on a phone. Six should-fix items, five nice-to-haves.

**Methodology page: absent.** No nav link (`a[data-nav="methodology"]`), no `#page-methodology`
element, and the word "methodology" does not appear anywhere in the rendered text. Not a
placeholder — entirely unbuilt. Consistent with the phased plan (PRD §11 build order, RPT-010
`passes: false`), but PRD §8 specifies five nav links, so the nav is currently four-of-five.

### Should-fix

#### S1 — Chart text renders at 5–6 px effective size on mobile; all axes unreadable

Every chart has a fixed `viewBox` width of 720 (or 980/1008/1120) scaled into a 358 px content
column, giving a uniform scale factor of **0.497**. Declared label sizes of 10–12 px therefore
render at **5–6 CSS px**, measured text-box heights of 7–8 px. Axis ticks, legends and in-chart
annotations are illegible at 390 px, and the effect is worse on the wider charts (Energy
Management `viewBox` 980 → scale 0.365; ground track 1120 → 0.32).

- Measured (Analysis → Overview, 390 px): svg[0] `effFontPx` 5.0, svg[1..2] 6.0, svg[3..7] 5.5.
  At 1440 px the same elements are 10–12 px.
- Evidence: `reviews/playwright/screenshots/evidence/ov-mobile-chart-labels-illegible-390.png`,
  `reviews/playwright/screenshots/crops/ov-mobile-b.png`

**Fix:** make label size scale-aware in the chart library — divide the intended px size by the
render scale, or emit a mobile `viewBox` variant. Simplest deterministic option: compute
`fontPx = base / (renderedWidth / viewBoxWidth)` is not available at build time, so instead set
chart label font-size in **CSS** (`svg text { font-size: 12px }` with `vector-effect`-style
non-scaling behaviour is not available for text) — the practical build-time fix is to emit a
second, narrower `viewBox` layout (e.g. 420-wide with fewer ticks and a stacked legend) behind a
`@media (max-width: 640px)` class swap, or to raise in-SVG label sizes to ~22–24 units so they
land near 11–12 px at the mobile scale while staying acceptable on desktop.

#### S2 — Axis tick labels clipped by the outer `<svg>` (UA `overflow: hidden`)

Text drawn outside the `viewBox` is clipped by the browser's default `overflow: hidden` on the
outer `<svg>`. Several charts place ticks past the edge. Measured overflow (px beyond the svg box):

| View | Chart (caption) | Label | Clipped by | Visible result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Overview | "Round 17 cumulative laps…" | y-tick `15` | 9.9 px top | renders as `--` |
| Overview | "Distance rounds in time order…" (progression) | right-axis `20` | 4.1 px top | half-height glyphs |
| Overview | "Average task speed vs average laps…" (scatter) | x-tick `30` | **8.4 px left** | renders as `)` |
| Overview | scatter | y-tick `12` | 3.0 px top | marginal |
| Round 1 | "Laps completed over the task…" | x-tick `40` | **27.8 px right** | **tick absent** |
| Round 3 | same | x-tick `40` | 69.8 px right | tick absent |
| Round 4 | same | x-tick `60` | 85.9 px right | tick absent |
| Round 5 | same | x-tick `40` | 56.9 px right | tick absent |
| Round 9 | score-ladder | `Bill 581` | 4.4 px bottom | glyph descenders cut |
| Round 9, 15, 17 | cumulative laps | top y-tick | 7.3–9.9 px top | renders as `--` |

- Evidence: `screenshots/evidence/ov-cumlaps-top-tick-clipped-1440.png`,
  `screenshots/evidence/ov-scatter-left-tick-clipped-1440.png`,
  `screenshots/evidence/r1-cumlaps-right-tick-missing-1440.png`

**Fix:** in the chart library, (a) pad the `viewBox` by the tick-label metrics (add ~12 px top
margin and half the widest tick-label width to the left/right margins), and (b) as a safety net add
`overflow: visible` to the chart `<svg>` rule. The right-edge case in the round cumulative-laps
chart is the worst — the last tick is the axis maximum, so the axis currently appears to stop short
of the data.

#### S3 — Legend text collides with the right-axis tick on the progression chart

On Analysis → Overview, "Progression Across The Week": the dual-axis chart's right-axis top tick
`20` is drawn on top of the `Laps` legend label (bounding boxes overlap by 53 % of the smaller
box — the only collision the automated pairwise-bbox sweep found across all 18 views at both
widths).

- Selector: Overview svg index 5, caption "Distance rounds in time order: within-group normalised
  score (solid) vs raw laps (dashed)"
- Evidence: `screenshots/evidence/ov-progression-legend-tick-collision-1440.png`

**Fix:** move the legend to a dedicated row above the plot area (as the other charts do with more
clearance), or reserve the top ~16 px of the plot box so the topmost tick and the legend cannot
share a band.

#### S4 — Chart widths are inconsistent inside one content column

The content column measures **1008 px** at desktop. Within a single round view the charts render
at three different widths: ground track **1008**, Energy Management **980**, cumulative laps
**720**. In the Overview, 19 of 20 charts are 720 and the ground track is 1008. The 720-wide
charts leave ~288 px of dead space on the right while the number-card rows and prose run the full
1008, producing a visibly ragged right edge down the page.

- Evidence: `screenshots/crops/ov-desktop-c.png` (720-wide bar charts vs full-width prose),
  `screenshots/crops/r12-desktop-b.png` (980-wide Energy Management immediately above a 720-wide
  cumulative-laps chart)

**Fix:** standardise the chart `viewBox` width on the content column (1008, or a single deliberate
narrower measure applied to *all* charts) so every figure shares one left and right edge.

#### S5 — Section controls overlap each other and the heading on mobile

In every collapsible section header at 390 px, the `.section-toggle` and the "back to top" link
occupy overlapping rectangles in the header row, and the link wraps mid-phrase ("Collapse  back"
/ "to top"). Measured on Analysis → Round 12: toggle `left 282, right 334, top 833, bottom 854`;
back-to-top `left 282, right 374, top 834, bottom 879` — overlapping in both axes, and both
overlapping the heading box (`16–374 × 807–907`). Reproduces on Recommendations and Innovations
too.

- Evidence: `screenshots/evidence/r12-mobile-section-controls-overlap-390.png`,
  `screenshots/crops/rec-mobile-a.png`

**Fix:** at mobile widths put the heading on its own row and the two controls on a second row
(`flex-direction: column` on `.section-head` under `@media (max-width: 640px)`), and add
`white-space: nowrap` to the back-to-top link.

#### S6 — Ground-track legend sits inside the plot, over the GPS traces

On every round's "Ground Track & Course" chart the Bill / Leader / Course legend is placed inside
the plot frame at bottom-right, where the leader's blue trace runs directly behind it. There is
also a ~110 px empty band below the trace inside the framed box (box is 1008 × 882 rendered), so
there is room to move the legend out.

- Evidence: `screenshots/evidence/r12-groundtrack-legend-overlap-1440.png`

**Fix:** move the legend into the empty band below the plot frame (or above it, matching the other
charts' legend position), and trim the frame's vertical padding.

### Nice-to-have

#### N1 — Scatter points fall outside the plotted y-range

On Analysis → Overview, "Field Position & The Laps/Speed Scatter", the y-axis bottom tick is `7`
but several field points are drawn below that gridline, in the axis margin alongside the x-tick
label row. The y-domain does not cover the data.
Evidence: `screenshots/crops/ov-desktop-d.png` (points near y ≈ 545 px vs the `7` gridline at
y ≈ 530 px).
**Fix:** extend the y-domain to `[min, max]` with a small pad before choosing ticks.

#### N2 — "Solar radiation" tile is an unlabelled colour block

The per-round dashboard's fourth tile is a plain terracotta rectangle with the caption "Relative to
all 17 rounds · lift proxy" — no value, no scale, no legend, so the reader cannot decode it. The
other three tiles in that row all carry a number.
Evidence: `screenshots/crops/r12-desktop-a.png`.
**Fix:** print the W/m² figure on the tile and add a two-ended scale hint (e.g. "weakest → strongest
of the week").

#### N3 — Heat-ramp colours are ad hoc, outside the design tokens

The solar-radiation tile and the Overview radiation strip use inline red→orange→yellow values
(`rgb(176,32,32)` … `rgb(250,224,139)`) with no matching `:root` token, while every other chart
colour comes from `--primary #86BC25` / `--leader #00A3E0` / `--field #D0D0CE` / `--attention
#ED8B00`.
**Fix:** define the sequential ramp as tokens so it is auditable and consistent.
*(The orange bars in "Gap Breakdown" and "Where The Ground Is Lost" do come from the defined
`--attention: #ED8B00` token, so they are deliberate — but PRD §8 names only green, cool greys and a
teal/blue secondary, so it is worth an explicit confirmation from the user that orange is in the
palette.)*

#### N4 — "Bill" is grey in two Overview charts but green everywhere else

The "Conditions Dependence" and "Consistency: Floor vs Ceiling" charts plot Bill's rounds in
`--field` grey, while `--field` grey is the "event winner / other pilots" colour in the
neighbouring charts. The semantics of grey flip between adjacent figures.
Evidence: `screenshots/crops/ov-desktop-e.png`, `screenshots/crops/ov-desktop-b.png`.
**Fix:** use `--primary` green for Bill's own points in those two charts.

#### N5 — Redundant in-SVG chart title on the Energy Management figure

The Energy Management chart draws "Energy Management" inside the SVG, immediately below the
identical section heading. No other chart does this.
Evidence: `screenshots/crops/r12-desktop-b.png`.
**Fix:** drop the in-SVG title; the section heading and the figcaption already carry it.
